# Finance Trigger Catalog

The stable catalog is FIN-T01 through FIN-T20. FIN-T01–06 are effective due stages (7d, 3d, tomorrow, today, exact-hour, overdue); only the highest applicable due stage is delivered per obligation. FIN-T07–10 cover statement review, missing obligation, minimum payment and unconfirmed scheduled payment. FIN-T11–12 cover recurrence. FIN-T13–14 are per-currency cash requirements; FIN-T15 consumes, but does not calculate, a future cashflow signal. FIN-T16–20 cover repeated nudges and special obligation types.
